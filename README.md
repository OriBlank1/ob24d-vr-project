**ABOUT THIS PROJECT**

This is a reflection of my room and things I think would also make great additions to my room. I added my usual stuff that exists in my room (bed, cabinets, TVs, gaming monitors, video games, posters, flags) in addition to a bookshelf and chess set. The room is meant to be cozy (much like my room in real life) and I added a fan with code produced by AI that makes the fan spin around.
